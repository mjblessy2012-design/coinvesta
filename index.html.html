🪙 Bitcoin (BTC)

Digital Gold

Bitcoin is the world's first decentralized cryptocurrency, introduced in 2009. It uses blockchain technology for secure peer-to-peer transactions without banks or intermediaries.

Key Features

  • ✔ World's First Cryptocurrency
  • ✔ Secure Blockchain Technology
  • ✔ Decentralized Digital Currency

Symbol : BTC

Founder : Satoshi Nakamoto

Launch Year : 2009
